# Ruby & Co. Loyalty Program — LINE Mini App

**Role:** Product Owner — scoping, spec, and business logic
**Deployed with:** engineering support from Nuttapon Pattanavijit (production deployment)
**Platform:** LINE Mini App

## Problem
Ruby & Co. needed a way to retain repeat customers post-launch without building a full loyalty stack from scratch. LINE Mini App was chosen as the delivery surface since it's where the brand's Thai customer base already lives.

## What I Did
- Scoped the loyalty SaaS concept: program mechanics, user flow, and business rules
- Defined the spec and requirements handed off for build
- Owned end-to-end product decisions on how the loyalty system should work within the Ruby & Co. customer journey
- Coordinated production deployment with an engineering partner

## Note on Collaboration
The engineering partner's role was deployment to production — scoping, spec, and logic design were owned by me as PO.

## Status
Scoped; deployed to production as part of the Ruby & Co. launch plan.
