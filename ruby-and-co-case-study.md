# Ruby & Co. — Pet-Safe Floor Cleaner Brand Website

**Role:** Founder / Product Owner — strategy, design direction, build, and launch
**Stack:** Vibe-coded with Claude / Claude Code, deployed on Cloudflare
**Live:** rubyandco.store

## Problem
The Thai pet-care market has plenty of floor cleaners, but none positioned specifically as pet-safe. Existing brands (Mypaws, SOGANICS, LAGA) compete on general cleaning claims — none own the "safe around pets" narrative. Ruby & Co. was built to claim that uncontested category.

## What I Did
- Ran competitive analysis against Mypaws, SOGANICS, and LAGA to identify the positioning gap
- Defined brand direction: Cormorant Garamond + DM Sans typography, earth-tone palette, layout inspired by Fetch & Co.
- Designed and built the site end-to-end using Claude/Claude Code, deployed on Cloudflare
- Scoped the hero SKU strategy: single hero product (BPT-0008, 500ml, ฿189) rather than splitting focus across SKUs
- Planned studio photoshoot direction using the brand's dog, Ruby, as the hero image
- Designed fulfillment plan: self-pack under 20 orders/month, switching to a 3PL (PACKHAI) past that threshold

## Decisions & Trade-offs
- **Single hero SKU over multiple:** simplifies the buying decision and marketing message for a first-time brand
- **Self-pack fulfillment threshold:** avoids premature 3PL cost before volume justifies it
- **Paused further investment (~฿40,000) to protect financial buffer** rather than push forward without margin for error — a deliberate PM call to de-risk timing over speed

## Status
Product testing in progress (4-household structured trial); launch planned for September.
